The numbers behind the dots

Lead = registered, male, 35+, from the US, Canada, UK, Australia or New Zealand. Income for 6 months = registrations × week-0 ARPU × return multiplier (8.3 desktop, 4 mobile). Partner №2 sends both desktop and mobile traffic, so its multiplier is blended by registrations: 733 desktop and 394 mobile, which gives ×6.80.

Every deal has a price window

A partner needs at least its cost per lead. We can pay at most our 6-month income per lead. Partner №1 desktop has no window at week-1 quality: the partner needs $8.12 and we can afford $8.00. Prices alone cannot fix it.

On CPA, conversion helps the partner

Raise landing conversion on a CPA campaign and our ROI stays flat, because income and cost both scale with leads. The partner's ROI climbs. On the CPC deal it works the other way round.

Our best campaign is about to quit

Partner №1 mobile returns +117% for us but loses 21% for the partner. Paying $5.00 instead of $3.50 keeps it alive and still returns about +52%.
